Observation — convention not yet finalised

The current practice embeds the SBU and License qualifiers inside the file-type segment (e.g. Recap Sample MAA Sport Licensed). The “COE Question” sheet of the naming workbook proposes a stricter order — [compcode]-[SBU Code]-[Brand Code]-[Season+YY]-[Inline/Licensed]-[Source File Type]-[Multi/Mono]-[Sequence] — which the sample above does not follow (brand name instead of brand code, full year instead of YY). Until the convention is ratified, filename classification must keep tolerating both variants.
